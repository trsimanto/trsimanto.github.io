About

I'm a Lead Mobile Developer with 9+ years building native Android, native iOS and Flutter apps end to end: architecture, API planning, testing, CI/CD, store release and production monitoring. I've shipped 70+ apps and spent 6+ years working remotely with teams in Romania, Finland, Estonia, Japan and the US.
